# Data sources

| Source | Read | Frequency |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Directory | group membership, employment status | hourly |
| Grant log | who granted what, when, and why | on demand |
| Ticket system | the justification referenced by a grant | on demand |

## Not read

Individual activity logs. Whether someone *used* access is a different question from
whether they should *have* it, and mixing the two produces reviews that rubber-stamp
heavy users and flag careful ones.
