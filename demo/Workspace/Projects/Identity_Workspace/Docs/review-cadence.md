# Review cadence

Quarterly for standing access, immediately for anything granted outside the normal path.

## What the review actually asks

Not "does this person still work here" — the directory answers that. The question is
whether the *reason* for the grant still holds. A grant made for a migration that finished
eight months ago is the thing these reviews exist to find.

## Output

A list of grants with no current justification, sent to the grant owner, not to the
person holding the access. The owner is who can revoke it; the holder has no incentive to.

Nothing is revoked automatically. An automatic revoke at 2am during an incident is a
second incident.
